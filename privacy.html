Tatik's Bakery: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Tatik's Bakery is a game for children and families. We built it so that we do not collect any personal information. The only outside service in the game is Google AdMob, which shows short videos a player can choose to watch for extra coins. This page explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends any information to us. The game uses the internet only for the optional videos: when it opens, it asks Google AdMob for one video in the background, so a video can start right away if a player taps to watch it (see Videos below). Apart from that, nothing leaves the device unless a grown-up chooses to send a postcard, described below.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: the day you reached, your coins, the dishes and words you learned, the decorations you chose and your settings such as language and sound. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

We collect no personal information, from children or anyone else. There are no chats, no links to websites inside the game, and no real-money purchases. The coins in the game are earned by playing or by choosing to watch a short video, and cannot be bought. There are no pop-up or banner ads, and nothing in the game ever requires watching a video.

Videos

In a few places a player can tap a button to watch a short video for a small reward: double the day's tips, 25 extra coins in the shop, one more daily chest, or one more watering of the lemon tree. A video plays only when the player taps that button, never during play, and at most 5 times a day.

The videos come from Google AdMob. We ask Google to treat every request as directed to children, to show only videos suitable for all ages (rated G), and never to personalize them. The game does not ask for permission to track you and does not use the advertising identifier for tracking. To show a video, prevent fraud and count views, Google may receive technical information such as the device's IP address, the device model and operating system, and whether the video was watched. We do not receive this information. Google's handling of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sending a postcard

A child can make a postcard: a picture of their decorated bakery. Before it can be sent, a grown-up has to answer a question. Only then does the phone's own share menu open, and the grown-up chooses where the picture goes, for example to a family member in Messages. The picture contains only the game's drawing and the day number. It is made on the device, it goes only where the grown-up sends it, and nothing is sent to us.

Third parties

The only third-party service in the game is Google AdMob, used for the optional videos described above, with child-directed, G-rated, non-personalized settings. The game includes no analytics or tracking services.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
